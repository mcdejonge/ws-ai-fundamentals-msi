# Sources

Sources for the claims and quotes in the slide deck `ai fundamentals.pptx`, listed per slide. Image sources are given on the slides themselves.

Most of the papers below are freely available on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org). You don't need to read them to follow the workshop; they are here in case you want to know more.

## Slide 3: What is intelligence?

- Seth, A. (2021). *Being You: A New Science of Consciousness*. Faber & Faber.
  An accessible book on the brain as a prediction machine. It is about consciousness rather than intelligence.

## Slide 5: Pattern detection using computers

- "AI is that which is not yet possible" is a paraphrase of what is known as *Tesler's theorem*: "AI is whatever hasn't been done yet". It is attributed to computer scientist Larry Tesler, who says what he actually said was "Intelligence is whatever machines haven't done yet": Tesler, L. (n.d.). Adages & coinages. <http://www.nomodes.com/larry-tesler-consulting/adages-and-coinages>

## Slide 7: The history of pattern recognition in computing

- Hamming, R. W. (1997). *The Art of Doing Science and Engineering: Learning to Learn*. Gordon and Breach.
- Deep learning (2012): Krizhevsky, A., Sutskever, I., & Hinton, G. E. (2012). ImageNet classification with deep convolutional neural networks. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 25*.
- Transformers (2017): Vaswani, A., et al. (2017). Attention is all you need. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 30*. <https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762>

## Slide 13: Deep learning: neural networks (1)

## Slide 22: Recent developments
